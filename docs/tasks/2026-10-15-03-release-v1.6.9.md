## Release notes

```markdown
## v1.6.9
- The page bar's size can be changed again; a page number in the bar now scales with the bar's height.
- Preferences ▸ Appearance has a Page Number section: show the number in the bar, above it, in the title bar, or not at all, and choose its font.
- New Page Bar option, Fill title bar: the title bar fills with a light shade as you read.
- The file-name bar at the bottom of the window has an opacity setting.
- In full screen the title bar shows the book's name again.
- Clicking OK in Preferences no longer resets an open book's reading direction or sort order, or sends it back to the first page.
- The page bar and other overlays stay in place when a window is tiled with Window ▸ Fill.
- Font previews in Preferences are readable in Light mode.
- Export keeps the original files' modification dates, and opening a book from Finder no longer replaces a window that is exporting.
```

If verification cannot confirm an item, tell the owner before replacing the
body.
